Privacy

Last updated: 2026-09-03

What Estafette stores

To deliver weekly PDFs, Estafette stores your Google account’s stable identifier, the ID of the Drive folder created for you, your connection status, delivery status, and an encrypted OAuth refresh token. Your token is never placed in GitHub Pages, GitHub Actions artifacts, or this repository.

Google Drive access

The application requests the narrow drive.file scope. This permits Estafette to create its delivery folder and manage the PDFs it creates there; it does not grant access to unrelated Drive content.

Disconnecting

Disconnecting revokes the stored authorization and removes the registration record. Files already delivered are not deleted from your Drive. You may also revoke Estafette from your Google Account’s connected-app settings.

Retention and contact

Registration data is retained while your connection remains active and is removed when you disconnect. Before public launch, replace this paragraph with the operator’s legal identity, contact address, retention policy, and any other notices required in the countries where the service operates.
