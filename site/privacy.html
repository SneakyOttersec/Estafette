Privacy / 2026

Plain terms.

Estafette asks for the smallest Drive permission it can use. Here is what is kept, what is never inspected, and what happens when you leave.

What Estafette stores

To deliver weekly PDFs, Estafette stores your Google account’s stable identifier, the ID of the Drive folder created for you, your connection and delivery status, and an encrypted OAuth refresh token. Your token is never placed in GitHub Pages, Actions artifacts, or this repository.

Google Drive access

The application requests the narrow drive.file scope. This permits Estafette to create its delivery folder and manage the PDFs it creates there; it does not grant access to unrelated Drive content.

Disconnecting

Disconnecting revokes the stored authorization and removes the registration record. Files already delivered are not deleted from your Drive. You may also revoke Estafette from your Google Account’s connected-app settings.

Retention and contact

Registration data is retained while your connection remains active and is removed when you disconnect. Before public launch, the operator must add their legal identity, contact address, retention policy, and locally required notices.
