WEEKLY / SECURITY / PDF

Security reading, relayed to your Drive.

Estafette gathers new posts from selected security blogs and builds a clean, e-reader-friendly PDF every week. Connect Google Drive once; each new edition lands in your own Estafette folder.

Connect my Google Drive →

The app only requests access to files and folders it creates.

How it works

  1. 01 Connect Drive. Approve the limited Google Drive permission.
  2. 02 Folder created. Estafette creates its own folder in your Drive.
  3. 03 Read weekly. New PDFs are published directly into that folder.

A deliberately small permission

Estafette uses Google’s drive.file permission. It cannot browse or change unrelated files in your Drive. Disconnecting stops future delivery; PDFs already delivered remain yours.
